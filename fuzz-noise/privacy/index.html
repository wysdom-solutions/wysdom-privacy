Privacy Policy

Fuzz Noise is built to be a quiet utility, and that extends to your data. This page explains, in plain language, what the app does and doesn't collect. No jargon, no surprises.

Overview

Fuzz Noise (“the app”, “we”) is a minimal ambient sound app for sleep and focus, published by Wysdom Solutions LLC (“Wysdom Solutions”). This policy explains, in plain language, how the app handles information when you use it. It applies to the Fuzz Noise app on Google Play and the App Store.

In short: your sound mix, volumes, timer, and purchase status stay on your device. There's no account and no backend we operate. The free tier shows banner ads through Google AdMob, and a one-time purchase removes them.

Information we collect

The app has no account and no sign-in. You can use every feature without giving us your name, email, or any profile information.

Fuzz Noise itself doesn't collect, store, or transmit personal data. Your sound mix, volume levels, timer settings, and premium-purchase status are stored locally on your device. We run no backend server, so this information is never sent to us — there's no account, no cloud backup, and no sync.

The only data that leaves your device is handled by Google AdMob (banner ads on the free tier) and the app stores (one-time purchases), on their own platforms — see below.

Advertising

The free version of Fuzz Noise shows a single banner ad served by Google AdMob. We never play audio ads and never interrupt your sounds — removing the banner is part of the one-time upgrade.

AdMob is operated by Google and is governed by Google's own privacy policy. When it serves ads, it may collect:

  • device identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID, iOS IDFA) — only if you allow tracking
  • approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • device type, operating system, and app version
  • ad-interaction data (impressions, clicks)

On iOS, the system asks whether the app may use your advertising identifier. If you decline, AdMob serves non-personalized ads and doesn't use your identifier — declining is completely fine. You can remove ads entirely with the one-time upgrade.

Purchases

Fuzz Noise offers one optional, one-time purchase that removes ads and lets your sounds keep playing with the screen off (this uses Android's wake-lock permission to keep audio running). It's processed by the platform's billing system — Google Play Billing on Android, the App Store on iOS:

  • we never see or store your payment details
  • the store confirms the purchase to the app; only an entitlement flag — whether you've upgraded — is stored on your device
  • “Restore purchase” works through your store account, so there's no separate Fuzz Noise account

See Google's and Apple's privacy policies for how they handle purchase data.

Analytics & crash data

None. Fuzz Noise doesn't use any analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDK. We don't measure how you use the app or build a profile of you.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask you to create an account or log in.
  • We don't access your microphone, camera, photo library, location, or contacts — Fuzz Noise uses no such permissions, and no audio is ever recorded.
  • We don't operate a backend, so there's nothing on our side to collect, store, or leak.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We never run audio ads or interrupt playback to advertise.

Children's privacy

Fuzz Noise is a general-audience utility and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your choices

You're in control:

  • limit ad personalization — or decline tracking on iOS — through your device settings
  • remove the banner entirely with the one-time upgrade
  • uninstall the app at any time; that permanently removes all local app data and stops any data processing

Your store-side purchase history stays with Google or Apple under their own policies, and “Restore purchase” brings your upgrade back if you reinstall.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, note it in the app's store listing. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy or Fuzz Noise? Email privacy@wysdomsolutions.com and we'll get back to you. Fuzz Noise is made by Wysdom Solutions LLC.
