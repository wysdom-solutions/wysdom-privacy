Support · Fuzz Noise

How can we help?

Most questions are answered below. If yours isn't, email us.

Common questions
How do I remove the banner ad?+

Get the one-time upgrade. It removes the banner permanently and lets your sounds keep playing with the screen off. It's a single purchase, and there's no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?+

If you've upgraded before (for example, on a new phone), open Settings and tap Restore purchase. Your upgrade is tied to your store account, so it comes back without paying again.

If nothing restores, make sure you're signed in to the same Google account you bought it with, then email us.

Why does the sound stop when I lock my phone?+

Playing with the screen off is part of the one-time upgrade. On the free version, audio pauses when the app goes to the background. Upgrade once and it keeps playing all night.

How does the sleep timer work?+

Tap Timer in the header and pick a length: 15 minutes, 30, 1 hour, 2 hours, or a custom time. When it's up, the audio fades out gently instead of cutting, so it won't wake you.

I'm not hearing any sound. What should I check?+

A few quick things:

· Make sure at least one sound tile is selected, and its volume slider in Active Sounds isn't at zero.
· Check your phone isn't on silent and the media volume is up.
· If you use Bluetooth, confirm it's connected to the right speaker or headphones.

Still silent? Email us with your phone model and Android version.

Can I play more than one sound at once?+

Yes. Tap as many tiles as you like. They layer together, and each one gets its own volume slider so you can balance the mix.

Which devices is it available on?+

Fuzz Noise will launch on Android and iOS at the same time, on Google Play and the App Store. It isn't out yet, so keep an eye on both stores for the release.

Still stuck?

Email us with your phone model and what's happening, and we'll help you sort it out.
