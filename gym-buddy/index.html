Gym Buddy Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 27, 2026

Overview

Gym Buddy is committed to protecting your privacy. This app is designed with privacy in mind - we collect minimal data and store everything locally on your device. Gym Buddy makes no network requests and includes no analytics or crash reporting.

Data Collection

Gym Buddy does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All workout data (exercises, sets, reps, weights, routines, and workout history) is stored locally on your device using a SQLite database.

Permissions

Gym Buddy requests no device permissions. It does not access the camera, photo library, location, contacts, microphone, or any other system resource.

Network Access

Gym Buddy makes no network requests of any kind. The app works fully offline and has no online features.

Third-Party Services

Gym Buddy uses no third-party services, SDKs, analytics, advertising networks, or crash reporting tools.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device in a SQLite database. This data is never transmitted to external servers. The app works completely offline.

Data Deletion

Uninstalling Gym Buddy from your device permanently removes all local data including your exercises, routines, workout history, and preferences. There is no account or cloud backup - uninstalling means complete data removal.

Children's Privacy

Gym Buddy does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app is designed for adult fitness enthusiasts.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
